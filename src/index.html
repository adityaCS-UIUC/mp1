Approval workflows

Make every tool decision clear.

Yentha gives employees a simple place to request an AI tool and gives reviewers the context they need to approve, reject, or request more information.

  • Consistent intake: Collect purpose, owner, data needs, and business justification with every request.
  • Faster reviews: Route each tool to the right business, IT, and security reviewers.
  • Clear history: Keep decisions and reviewer notes together for future audits and renewals.

The problem Yentha solves

AI tools are easy to adopt, but difficult to govern.

New AI tools often enter a workspace faster than teams can document them. The result is a scattered portfolio with unclear ownership, inconsistent approvals, and several products solving similar problems.

  • Fragmented visibility: Tool information lives across receipts, browser tabs, spreadsheets, and team conversations.
  • Repeated capabilities: Different products may cover the same writing, design, research, or coding needs.
  • Unclear accountability: Without owners and review dates, approvals and lifecycle decisions are easy to lose.

Yentha's role: Bring tools, capabilities, approvals, owners, and review dates into one understandable workspace.
